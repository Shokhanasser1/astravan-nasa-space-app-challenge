Building interiors (habitat, greenhouse, lab/workshop, lander): modelled from scratch in Blender 5.3 by Claude Code
(AI-made, team-directed), 15 Nov 2026, for HOME BY SEVEN. Layout, sizes and colours from NASA technical reports (NTRS)
and the NASA Image and Video Library, each fact listed with its API request and document id in
blender/interiors/<building>/SOURCES.md. No NASA imagery, logos or insignia are in the models.
Not endorsed by NASA.

hdu_habitat.glb (15 Nov 2026, `?hdu`): the furnished interior of the HDU habitat (eight sections after NASA's HDU papers
on NTRS: GeoLab glovebox with three pass-through chambers, lettuce racks, telerobotics, medical, galley, workshop,
hygiene, the entry with suits; the X-Hab crew loft with four pods; a centre ladder), modelled by Blender Python written
with Claude Code (AI-made, team-directed) in oy-karvonsaroyi-3d/hb7/hdu_base/build_game_habitat.py (SOURCES.md there).
