astronaut.glb — the playable kid astronaut (skinned, 23 bones, 15 clips: Idle/Walk/Run/Carry/Pour/Jump/JumpStart/JumpAir/
JumpLand/Insert/Wrist/LookAround/Climb/Seated/SeatedSteer) and a small Battery prop in the right glove.
Body (15 Nov 2026): an AI-generated, textured 3D model — Meshy multi-image-to-3D, run on Higgsfield — made from the
team's concept picture (front, side and back views of a kid astronaut). The team's rig was fitted to its proportions
and the body skinned in Blender (scripts/models/astronaut_ai.mjs, blender/kid_astronaut/bind_ai.py). AI-made, disclosed
in docs/AI-USE.md. No NASA mesh, texture, insignia or flag.
Skeleton and clips: made by the team (blender/game3d/characters/, anim/).
