astronaut.glb — the playable kid astronaut (skinned, 23 bones, 15 clips: Idle/Walk/Run/Carry/Pour/Jump/JumpStart/JumpAir/
JumpLand/Insert/Wrist/LookAround/Climb/Seated/SeatedSteer) and a small Battery prop in the right glove.
Body (15 Nov 2026): a kid in a white suit with gold bands, chest pack and hoses, gold-visor bubble helmet and a compact
backpack, modelled from scratch in Blender 5.3 by script (blender/kid_astronaut/build_kid.py) after the team's concept
picture, and skinned onto the team's rig by scripts/models/astronaut_kid.mjs. AI-made (Claude Code), team-directed.
No NASA mesh, texture, insignia, flag or text in it.
Skeleton and clips: made by the team (blender/game3d/characters/, anim/), first fitted to NASA 3D Resources'
"Astronaut" model, which this file no longer contains.
