HOME BY SEVEN - the HDU outpost (?outpost), 2026-11-15

outpost.glb: three NASA Habitat Demonstration Unit modules joined by two pressurised tunnels, exterior only.
- Modules: "Habitat Demonstration Unit" part 1 (shell, side module, airlock) and part 2 (skid, fins, ramps, work
  porch), NASA 3D Resources: https://science.nasa.gov/3d-resources/habitat-demonstration-unit/
  GitHub: https://github.com/nasa/NASA-3D-Resources/tree/master/3D%20Models/Habitat%20Demonstration%20Unit
  Scaled from inches to metres; the NASA logo, the US flags and the X-Hab logo painted out of the texture (greyscale);
  part 2's fins cut at the shell wall; doors and tunnel lanes cut. NASA media guidelines; not endorsed by NASA.
- Tunnels: team-made in Blender (AI-written Blender Python, Claude Code).
Made by Samandar (team) with Claude Code in Blender (scripts: the team's oy-karvonsaroyi-3d/hb7/hdu_base,
build_base.py, export_outpost.py, SOURCES.md); web-converted with gltf-transform as in `npm run glb` (meshopt, WebP textures <= 1024) plus
--join false --flatten false: one node per module part and tunnel, so each sits on its own ground.
The furnished interiors (NASA HDU papers on NTRS; Moonwards-Assets toilet, MIT) stay in the Blender file.
