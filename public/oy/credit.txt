public/oy — the team's third-person Moon demo (Oy karvonsaroyi engine), static files outside the Vite app.
3D models: NASA 3D Resources / NASA Science (NASA/JPL-Caltech): HDU, SEV, RASSOR, crew lock bag, Earth globe;
astronaut from the NASA EMU model, rigged and animated in Blender. Logos, flags and lettering painted out.
Skies: made in Blender from NASA imagery (Blue Marble, SVS CGI Moon Kit, Viking). Not endorsed by NASA.
Terrain (terrain/site.json): heights from NASA LRO LOLA, NASA GSFC Planetary Geodesy Data Archive (Site01 5 m/px,
80-90S 20 m/px; Barker et al. 2021, 2023), built by scripts/data/moon_terrain.py. Rocks, prints, dust: code (look/).
Code: Claude Code agents for Samandar (see docs/AI-USE.md). Three.js loaded from cdn.jsdelivr.net (MIT).
