## What was built

- `public/data/facts.json` — 17 facts. Per fact: `id`, `value`, `unit`, `label` (measured / computed / rule / story,
  `src/lib/labels.ts`), `key` = `data.fact.<id>` (the sentence), `source` {title, url}, `quote` (the words on that
  page), `calc` for computed facts, optional `note` and `where` (which screen or file uses it).
- `src/i18n/{en,uz,ru}/data.json` — the 17 `data.fact.*` keys with empty values.
- `src/lib/facts.test.ts` (runs in `npm test`) — unique ids, unit, label, `data.fact.<id>` key present in all three
  languages, https source, quote, `calc` for computed facts, no banned wording, and `calmDose` / `stormPeak` equal to
  what `src/engine/weeks.json` holds (a data rebuild that changes them turns the test red).
- Every source URL was fetched on 14 Nov 2026 and the number was found on the page (quoted in `quote`).
  Kept out on purpose: the station and orbit words the team dropped (CLAUDE.md, Wording), endorsement wording,
  NASA logos.

Notes for the writer:
- `calmDose` and `stormPeak` are measured **in lunar orbit** behind thin shielding: never say "at the base".
- `levelStep` is a **game rule** (the 2 / 10 / 100 / 1,000 cut points) shaped like NOAA's scale; the line must not
  say the levels are measured.
- `donkiFlare` names a date: show it on the Data page only (the date is never shown during play).
- `earthSize`: "four Moons wide" compares with the full Moon seen from Earth (3.7×).

## Left out or not confirmed

- **Apollo 15 hammer and feather** (verified: [NSSDCA](https://nssdc.gsfc.nasa.gov/planetary/lunar/apollo_15_feather_drop.html),
  1.32 kg hammer, ~1.6 m, landed together) — left out because the Day 1 gravity guess is not built; ready to add.
- **Earth 2–6° above the horizon** (sky.json, week 2021-10-25) — JPL Horizons was not reachable from this sandbox
  (TLS error), and sky.json covers one week only. Not in facts.json.
- **Moon radius 1737.4 km** — on the fact sheet, but no screen uses it.
- **MODIS every 1–2 days** ([terra.nasa.gov](https://terra.nasa.gov/about/terra-instruments/modis)) — verified, but
  the week-1 Earth images are VIIRS (manifest.json), so the fact names VIIRS. Weeks built later from MODIS need a
  second line.
- **Flare letters (X = 10 × M)** and the **shelter / water-wall basis** (NASA TB-020) — no source page fetched; not added.
- **Data-lane note:** RadLab returns exactly `50000.0` µGy/h for some hours (e.g. 2012-01-24 00:07, which the
  weeks.json bucketing puts in day 4, max 50 000). It may be an instrument ceiling; the data lane should check before
  showing per-hour maxima. RadLab went down (HTTP 502) during this check, so `stormPeak` was re-fetched for 23 of
  the 24 hours of 24 Jan 2012; with weeks.json's day mean (32 395 µGy/h) they imply ~13 000 µGy/h for the missing
  hour, in line with the falling readings before it.
