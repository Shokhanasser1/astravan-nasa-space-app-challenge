## Data method (weeks.json)

- Normal = 25th percentile of the hourly readings in the 27 days before the week. The concept's own normal is not
  written down; this choice reproduces its week-1 ratios (1.0 1.0 1.0 9.2 42 25 8.5 → ours 1.03 1.02 1.04 9.19
  41.88 24.19 8.49) and all 14 week kinds. Samandar/Sarvar: if your `weeks.json` used another rule, say so.
- Alert = X-class flare that day, or the week's first particle event. Watch = before the first storm day, a
  front-side CME ≥ 600 km/s (|longitude| ≤ 90°) or an M5+ flare. Week 1: watch day 2, alert day 4 — as in the concept.
- `hourly`: 24 ratios per day for the dosimeter and the truth curve.

## Rule decisions the documents leave open (check against rules_engine.py)

1. Ice "Go" with 1 Explore battery = a long walk (reserve −1); with 2–3 batteries it is not a second −1.
2. Filter "Wait": reserve −1 from the same day (Dust "Leave" says "from tomorrow"; Filter does not).
3. Card prices (Brush, Swap, Heat) take one of today's batteries.
4. A storm card (alert or level ≥ 1) replaces the day card; Watch is only a radio line next to the day card.
5. Shelter choice on every alert or storm day, day 7 included (Plan C reproduces only with "half" on day 7).
6. Emergency day: reserve ≤ 0 → reserve 0, next day forced: Launch 4 first on day 7, then 3 Life Support, 1 Garden,
   everyone inside; crew −2. The forced day has no card.
7. Late ride: Launch < 4 on day 7 → title "Late Ride Home", crew −2.
8. Hard-mode weeks use the severe thresholds (crew 6, science 11).
9. Plans B and C are reconstructed from their one-line description; both reproduce the concept's totals exactly.
