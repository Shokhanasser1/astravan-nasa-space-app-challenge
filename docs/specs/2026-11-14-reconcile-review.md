# Spec: reconcile our research with the team's documents — keep, remove, re-verify, propose

## Goal

HOME BY SEVEN gets the best of everything the team has learned, nothing that can cost points, and the freshest
facts: our earlier research (Oy karvonsaroyi: NASA data, kids' design research, critics' scores, winners and
competitors) is merged with the team's PDFs, every risky line is found and replaced, every key fact is re-checked
online today, and new useful data or materials are proposed — each with an owner and a gate.

## Inputs

- Our work: `E:\portfolio\hackaton\docs\Oy karvonsaroyi итоговый свод команды.md` (summary), critics' rounds
  (`idea_v5_variants_RU.md`, `idea_v6_RU.md`, `idea_v7_RU.md`: v3 58 → v7 84/100; NASA rubric Impact 3, Creativity 4,
  Validity 3, Relevance 4, Presentation 4), `nasa_judging_2025_rubric.md`, research notes (winners, kids' design,
  competitors, code, art, NASA data and dose method), `havolalar.md`.
- Team: final version, concept v2, concept 3.0, Dizayn 2.0, scenario, developer guide, work plan, Sunday plan.
- Online, today: Space Apps 2026 Project Submission Guide and Judging & Awards Guide (published 13 Nov), the
  challenge's Resources tab, NASA media/AI guidelines, Artemis and Artemis II status and any published radiation
  data, the data endpoints we use (RadLab, DONKI, SDO, GIBS, Horizons), competitors' current state.

## Output: `docs/REVIEW.md`

1. **Keep and add** — what our research proved and the team documents lack or weaken (e.g. measured storm hardness:
   a loud storm is not always the dangerous one; "N out of 20" frequencies instead of percent; before/after evidence
   from real children with a published number; a school/teacher letter for Impact; method page for Validity).
2. **Remove or rewrite** — every line in the team documents or code that risks points or rules: quoted line,
   where it is, which rule/guide it breaks, the safe replacement.
3. **Critics' lessons** — what lowered past scores and whether HOME BY SEVEN fixes it; what is still open.
4. **Re-verified facts** — each key fact with source link and "checked 2026-11-14"; anything that changed.
5. **New proposals** — new data or materials found online, ranked by (points gained ÷ hours), each with owner,
   gate (before G2 / after G2 / Sunday / pitch only), and what is cut if it does not fit.

## Acceptance

- Every claim has a link or a file reference; nothing from memory alone.
- Every "remove" quotes the exact line and names the rule; every proposal names an owner and a gate.
- No proposal reopens a fixed final-version decision without saying so explicitly.
- Fits the timeline: anything not doable by Sunday 11:00 is marked "pitch only" or "after the hackathon".

## Out of scope

Writing game text (team writes it), changing the engine rules, implementing the proposals (each becomes its own
spec after the team agrees).
