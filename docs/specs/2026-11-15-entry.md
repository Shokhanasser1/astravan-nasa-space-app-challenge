# Entry: the reception desk (15 Nov 2026, Asilbek)

Behind `features.entry` (`true`). Cut → the old start screen (Start / Continue, one shared save).

## Idea

Like a hotel: you meet the reception desk first (what this place is, why it exists), check in, then go to your
room (the Moon base). Two ways in, as the team asked:

| | Cadet badge (long-term) | Guest pass (short-term) |
|---|---|---|
| What is kept | Missions flown, titles earned, the game in progress | Nothing |
| Story | Mission 1 is always the first-mission week (25–31 Oct 2021), then new real weeks; ranks grow | One mission (the first-mission week or the class code) |
| Who | A call sign picked from a list + a patch colour | Nobody |

A guest who finishes is offered to keep the title on a new badge (common "guest first, account later" pattern).

## Hard limits it keeps (CLAUDE.md "Children")

- No name, age, e-mail, face, photo, voice or date is asked or stored. A call sign is **picked**, never typed
  (`lochin, yulduz, humo, burgut, kamalak, shunqor`); one per device, at most 6 badges (a shared school laptop).
- Everything stays in this browser (`src/lib/storage.ts`, keys `profiles`, `lastBadge`, `game:<id>`,
  `played:<id>`). Nothing goes to a server; `features.sendResults` stays false.
- Storage that is missing, broken or edited by hand is cleaned (`sanitize`) and never breaks the game.

Asilbek, 15 Nov ~05:30–06:00: "for kids — fewer words, beautiful colours, professional buttons; the best 3D of the
game behind it; different at first sight". Built like the living menus of big games (the menu shows the game
itself): **the 3D base is the background**, filmed by a slow title camera (`src/scene/title.ts`, used by

menu sit at the edge over a dark scrim: left column on wide screens, title on top and a bottom sheet on tall
screens. No voice on the desk.

A returning cadet lands in their lobby (`lastBadge`).

## Ranks (game rule, the team may change)

Cadet 0 missions · Crew member 1 · Mission specialist 3 · Commander 5 (`RANKS` in `src/profile/profiles.ts`).

## Research it follows

- Kids' services: guest play with no personal data; account (here: badge) later; no age gate as a shield.
- Shared devices: profile pickers (one tile per child) instead of passwords.
- "Check-in" onboarding framed as a place in the game world (Animal Crossing's airport counter).
