## Maxsus tekshiruvlar (jamoa so'ragan)

### Mount Hadley tekislikdan qancha baland? (studiya: ~1.5 km)
**Javob:** ~4.5 km. Studiyadagi ~1.5 km XATO.  
Apollo 15 Preliminary Science Report (NASA SP-289), 5-17-rasm: 'Mount Hadley (approximately 4.5 km high)'. NASA 3D/rasm sahifasidagi 'about 4,765 feet' (≈1.45 km) — 14,765 ft (=4.5 km) dagi '1' tushib qolgan xato bo'lishi ehtimoli katta.  
Manbalar: https://www.nasa.gov/history/alsj/a15/a15psrf5-17.html · https://science.nasa.gov/3d-resources/moon-mount-hadley/ · https://www.nasa.gov/image-article/mount-hadley/

### apollo-11-site relyefi necha marta bo'rttirilgan? (studiya: 60×)
**Javob:** 60× — TO'G'RI. Hudud 30 × 30 km.  
NASA 3D sahifasi: 'Height map of the area around the Apollo 11 landing site (30x30 km). Exaggerated 60 times in the Z axis.'  
Manbalar: https://science.nasa.gov/3d-resources/apollo-11-landing-site/

### NASA HDU va 'Deep Space Habitat' bir xil qurilmami?
**Javob:** Ha — bir xil HDU qobig'i, faqat har xil jihozlangan: 2010 — PEM, 2011–2012 — DSH (HDU-DSH).  
NASA 3D sahifasi DSH ni 'HDU-DSH shell' deb ataydi; NASA 2012 rasm izohi: '2011 version of the deep space habitat ... includes the Habitat Demonstration Unit with the student-built X-Hab loft on top'. NTRS: 2010 da HDU 'PEM' sifatida sinalgan. hdu-1 / hdu-2 = bitta modelning 'part 1' / 'part 2' fayllari.  
Manbalar: https://science.nasa.gov/3d-resources/habitat-demonstration-unit/ · https://www.nasa.gov/image-article/deep-space-habitat-x-hab-loft/ · https://ntrs.nasa.gov/citations/20110013501

### Crew Lock Bag va Cargo Transfer Bag o'lchamlari
**Javob:** CTB (bitta): 16¾ × 19¾ × 9¼ dyuym ≈ 42.5 × 50.2 × 23.5 sm, 27 kg gacha (STS-92 press kit, tasdiqlangan). CLB: ochiq manbada o'lcham TOPILMADI (null).  
Ular har xil sumkalar: CTB — XKS yuk sumkasi; CLB — EVA asbob sumkasi. NASA 'water wall' tadqiqotlari CTB larni suv bilan to'ldirishni o'rgangan, CLB ni emas.  
Manbalar: https://www.nasa.gov/wp-content/uploads/2023/05/sts-092-press-kit.pdf · https://ntrs.nasa.gov/api/citations/20160006051/downloads/20160006051.pdf · https://science.nasa.gov/3d-resources/crew-lock-bag/

### Gateway hozirgi holati (PPE + HALO, 2026)
**Javob:** 2026-03-24: NASA Gateway'ni 'hozirgi shaklda to'xtatish'ni e'lon qildi. PPE → SR-1 Freedom (2028 oxiri, Mars). HALO → Oy yuzasidagi 3 ta texnologik namoyish. Hech narsa uchirilmagan.  
NASA 3D sahifasidagi 'Artemis IV dan oldin' degan matn eskirgan.  
Manbalar: https://www.nasa.gov/news-release/nasa-unveils-initiatives-to-achieve-americas-national-space-policy/ · https://www.nasa.gov/mission/space-reactor-1-freedom/ · https://www.nasa.gov/missions/moon-base/nasa-provides-updates-on-moon-base-cargo-landers-tech-demonstrations/
